# Time-based audits

## Pre-purchase audit

Block `READY TO BUY` until applicable checks pass:

- traveler identity, nationality, passport validity, and entry/transit requirements;
- exact dates, effective return date, nights, timezones, and hard constraints;
- essential event availability and dependency on arrival;
- protected flight itinerary or explicitly accepted separate-ticket risk;
- baggage and fare family in both directions;
- seller, operator, taxes, mandatory fees, payment currency, changes, cancellation, and no-show;
- lodging coverage and arrival-compatible check-in;
- current ground transport feasibility and sales windows;
- insurance dates and material exclusions;
- consolidated per-person and group totals with dated conversion and contingency;
- simultaneous availability of dependent components;
- Plan B and explicit human approval.

Produce a purchase brief, not a transaction. State the maximum observed total and how long the evidence is expected to remain useful.

## 30 days before departure

- Recheck passport, visa/authorization, transit, EES/ETIAS/equivalent, health, and insurance.
- Confirm every essential reservation remains active and names/dates match.
- Recheck schedule changes, terminals, stations, works, strikes, closures, holidays, and timed entry.
- Buy eligible transport or entry only through a separate human-authorized action outside this skill.
- Confirm airport/station transfers and late check-in.
- Reconcile paid versus projected budget and emergency funds.
- Prepare encrypted/offline document copies, contacts, and shared itinerary.
- Confirm connectivity, payment, accessibility, medication, and Plan B arrangements.

## 7 days before departure

- Check official alerts, strikes, disruptions, event status, and near-term weather.
- Confirm check-in windows, baggage, schedules, terminals, stations, and transfer pickup.
- Confirm lodging arrivals and special requests.
- Download tickets, maps, policy, reservations, and addresses offline.
- Activate or prepare eSIM/roaming; check plugs, chargers, and batteries.
- Enable cards, review fees, local-currency payment rule, cash, and backup payment.
- Finalize medication, clothing, accessibility support, and luggage ownership.
- Share current plan and emergency contacts with travelers.

## 24 hours before departure

- Check first transport operational status and check in when available.
- Verify boarding documents, terminal, baggage, and departure-from-home time.
- Match passport to every electronic authorization and reservation name.
- Confirm offline access to the first 48 hours of bookings and addresses.
- Carry medication, chargers, payment methods, initial cash, and emergency contacts.
- Trigger Plan B immediately if a material disruption appears.

## Audit output

For each checkpoint report:

```text
Checkpoint:
Completed at:
```
