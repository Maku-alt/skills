# Specialist routing

Delegate the smallest independent question set. One worker may cover adjacent domains only when splitting would duplicate the same evidence.

| Specialty | Delegate when | Required output emphasis | Preferred sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Borders and documents | International travel, transit, minors, dual nationality, visas, stay limits, ETIAS/EES/ETA/eVisa. | Per-traveler applicability, document validity, transit countries, launch status, next review. | Government, immigration authority, consulate, official supranational portal. |
| Health and insurance | Vaccines, medication, pre-existing conditions, adventure activities, policy comparison. | Official health requirements versus recommendations; dates, limits, exclusions, assistance process. | Health ministry, WHO-equivalent authority, insurer policy wording. |
| Aviation | Multicity, codeshares, separate tickets, complex transit, baggage, fare families. | Effective operator/seller, protection, local times, baggage, total checkout scope, change rules. | Airline and airport first; regulator for passenger rights. |
| Ground transport | Rail, bus, ferry, rental car, border crossings, sales windows, connections. | Official operator, timetable validity, seats, luggage, connection protection, door-to-door comparison. | Operator, infrastructure manager, transport authority. |
| Lodging | Multiple cities, late arrivals, safety/accessibility, taxes, unclear cancellation. | All nights covered, final price, room, location, check-in, cancellation, direct versus reseller. | Property/operator and local tourism/tax authority; platform for quoted terms. |
| Events and timed entry | Concerts, sports, festivals, museums, tours, scarce slots. | Event occurrence separate from traveler ticket; official inventory, doors, restrictions, transport. | Venue, organizer, official ticketing channel, attraction. |
| Finance | Multiple currencies, group splits, DCC, fees, exchange rates, contingency. | Original currencies, dated conversion, paid/quoted split, emergency funds, card strategy. | Central bank/reference provider and bank fee schedules. |
| Itinerary and safety | Many bases, night arrivals, daylight, closures, strikes, weather, pace. | Zone grouping, door-to-door time, closures, official alerts, triggers and Plan B. | Official destination, transport, weather, security, and venue sources. |
| Accessibility | Mobility, sensory, cognitive, dietary, medical, or fatigue needs. | Exact barrier, assistance process, lead time, accessible alternatives, unresolved confirmation. | Operator/venue accessibility service and traveler-specific requirements. |
| Local agency or DMC | Group, complex cross-border plan, language/support need, high-value coordination. | Added value, scope, fees, licensing/reputation, emergency support, direct-booking comparison. | Official registry, agency contract, destination authority. |

## Source rules

- Prefer the source with authority over the exact rule or service, not merely the most prominent search result.
- Use aggregators for discovery and comparison, never as sole proof of border rules or provider confirmation.
- Distinguish operator from seller and official reseller from unaffiliated reseller.
- Cite near the claim and preserve the consultation date.
- When official sources conflict, report the conflict and route to the authority controlling entry, boarding, or service fulfillment.

## Decisions that must stay with travelers

Do not delegate final choice of budget ceiling, arrival deadline, risk tolerance, pace, room standard, insurance tradeoff, irreversible fare, or consent to sensitive-data use. Specialists may frame alternatives and consequences only.
