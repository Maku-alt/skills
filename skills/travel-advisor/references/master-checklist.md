# Master checklist

Apply proportionally. Mark each control `PASS`, `PARTIAL`, `MISSING`, `STALE`, `NOT APPLICABLE`, or `CONFLICTING`, with evidence and next action.

| # | Control | Pass condition |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Travelers and needs | Names, nationality, residence, age/DOB as needed, emergency contact, medical/dietary/accessibility needs, and pace preferences are known per traveler. |
| 2 | Dates and constraints | Departure, effective return, flexibility, nights, timezones, work/personal limits, and immovable events agree. |
| 3 | Passports, visas, borders | Citizenship, document issue/expiry, visa/permit rules, transit rules, stay limits, and supporting documents are checked per traveler. |
| 4 | Electronic border systems | EES, ETIAS, ETA, ESTA, eVisa, arrival cards, or equivalents have applicability, operational status, cost, and review date. |
| 5 | Vaccines, health, insurance | Official health advice, medication, declarations, pre-existing conditions, assistance, repatriation, dates, and exclusions are addressed. |
| 6 | Flights and connections | Exact airports, local times, operators, codeshares, terminals, connection protection, separate tickets, airport changes, and recovery plan are known. |
| 7 | Fare and baggage | Cabin/checked allowance, dimensions, shared baggage, seats, fare family, no-show, changes, cancellation, and total price are known. |
| 8 | Rail, bus, ferry, vehicles | Operators, schedules, sales windows, seats, luggage, connections, licenses, tolls, parking, fuel, and one-way fees are addressed as applicable. |
| 9 | Official operators and resellers | Effective operator and seller are distinct; support, fees, fulfillment, and official-channel alternatives are compared. |
| 10 | Lodging | All nights covered; room/occupancy, location, safety, check-in, reception, luggage storage, taxes, payment, and cancellation are known. |
| 11 | Realistic itinerary | Activities are grouped geographically with door-to-door travel, meals, rest, daylight, jet lag, and recovery time. |
| 12 | Closures and holidays | Weekly closures, public holidays, maintenance, seasonal hours, strikes, and reservation windows are checked. |
| 13 | Tickets, tours, guides, passes | Official inventory, time slot, language, inclusions, meeting point, cancellation, and pass break-even fit the real itinerary. |
| 14 | Local agencies | Value, licensing/reputation, emergency support, scope, markup, cancellation, and direct-booking alternative are evaluated when useful. |
| 15 | DMC or transversal agency | Cross-country coordination value is assessed for groups, complex logistics, accessibility, events, or high support needs. |
| 16 | Money and payments | Destination currencies, card acceptance, cash needs, ATMs, DCC, bank spreads, foreign fees, and backup cards are addressed. |
| 17 | Budget per person and group | Shared and individual costs, per-person total, group total, paid, committed, quoted, and remaining amounts are separate. |
| 18 | Exchange rate | Every conversion preserves original currency, source, rate, date, and rounding rule. |
| 19 | Contingency and emergency funds | Trip contingency and inaccessible-to-routine-spend emergency funds are explicit and adequate. |
| 20 | Connectivity and power | Roaming/eSIM/SIM, coverage, calls, offline access, plugs, voltage, chargers, adapters, and battery are covered. |
| 21 | Weather, clothing, packing | Seasonal conditions, near-term forecast, activity-specific clothing, laundry, baggage limits, and packing ownership are coherent. |
| 22 | Safety and emergencies | Official advisories, scams, night arrivals, neighborhoods, emergency numbers, consular contacts, and lost-document response are documented. |
| 23 | Accessibility and physical pace | Distances, stairs, surfaces, seating, toilets, breaks, assistance requests, sensory needs, and recovery capacity match travelers. |
| 24 | Events and concerts | Event, venue, date/time, doors, ticket status, restrictions, transport, and post-event return are separately verified. |
| 25 | Document copies and storage | Encrypted and offline copies, shared access, least-sensitive storage, recovery plan, and booking index exist. |
| 26 | Purchase order | Scarcity, dependencies, reversibility, cancellation windows, price volatility, and approval order determine sequence. |
| 27 | Changes and cancellation | Refund method, deadlines, penalties, name rules, no-show propagation, and intermediary support are understood. |
| 28 | Plan B | Delay, missed connection, strike, illness, closure, severe weather, lost baggage, and payment failure have triggers and responses. |
| 29 | Sharing the plan | Travelers know where the current plan lives, who owns each action, how changes are announced, and what remains available offline. |
| 30 | Final reviews | Pre-purchase, 30-day, 7-day, and 24-hour audits are scheduled or completed at the appropriate time. |

## Cross-check rules

Create a finding when any of these occur:

- itinerary nights do not equal lodging nights;
- a segment arrives after check-in or before a fixed event without adequate margin;
- an activity is scheduled during closure or before arrival;
- a separate ticket lacks baggage, transit, and recovery analysis;
- a transit country was omitted from document research;
- a policy ends before the effective return;
- a price lacks traveler count, currency, taxes, baggage, mandatory fees, or quote date;
- a local currency lacks a payment strategy;
- a critical task lacks owner, dependency, or deadline;
- an accepted risk lacks named decision maker and trigger for reopening;
- a claim says `confirmed` but evidence supports only `DECIDED`, `RESEARCHED`, or `QUOTED`.
